Cryogenic Freeze-Out & Resonant Self-Heating Dynamics

Complete mathematical formulation governing cryogenic electrolyte ionic mobility collapse, DC-DC bus capacitor equivalent series resistance (ESR) divergence, and closed-loop electro-thermal stabilization.

01 / Ionic Conductivity Transport Collapse

Vogel-Fulcher-Tammann (VFT) Ionic Mobility Model

In organic carbonate and polymer electrolytes used in flight-grade wet tantalum and polymer hybrid electrolytic capacitors, ionic conductivity $\sigma(T)$ does not follow standard Arrhenius linearity near sub-zero temperatures. Instead, cooperatively rearranging solvent cages cause viscosity to diverge exponentially as temperature approaches the ideal glass transition temperature $T_0$:

$$\sigma(T) = \sigma_0 \cdot \exp\left( - \frac{B}{k_B (T - T_0)} \right) = \frac{A}{\sqrt{T}} \exp\left( - \frac{E_v}{R_g (T - T_g)} \right)$$

Where $\sigma_0$ is the high-temperature conductivity limit, $B$ is the pseudo-activation energy describing ionic hopping barriers, $k_B$ is the Boltzmann constant, and $T_0$ is the divergence temperature ($T_0 \approx T_g - 45\text{ K}$). Below $T_g$, the electrolyte freezes into an amorphous glass, plunging mobility by over $4$ orders of magnitude.

Critical Boundary: $T_g = -130^\circ\text{C}$ ($143.15\text{ K}$) Lithium-TFSI / GBL Solvate
02 / Equivalent Series Resistance Bus Destabilization

Filter Capacitor Cryogenic Impedance Formulation

The total equivalent series resistance $R_{\text{ESR}}$ is the sum of intrinsic metallic lead/foil resistance $R_m$, oxide dielectric loss $R_{ox}(\omega)$, and cryogenic electrolyte solution resistance $R_{elyte}(T)$. As ionic conductivity collapses, $R_{elyte}(T) \propto 1/\sigma(T)$ utterly dominates the capacitor impedance:

$$R_{\text{ESR}}(T, \omega) = R_m + \frac{\tan \delta_{ox}}{\omega C} + \frac{d_{\text{sep}}}{A_{\text{eff}} \cdot \sigma_0} \exp\left( \frac{B}{k_B(T - T_0)} \right)$$

DC-DC converters rely on low $R_{\text{ESR}}$ to maintain output filter pole-zero placement. When $R_{\text{ESR}}$ exceeds $R_{\text{crit}} = 250\text{ m}\Omega$, the control loop phase margin degrades by $>65^\circ$, causing catastrophic voltage ringing, superconducting bus trips, and avionics brownouts.

Phase Margin Shift: $\Delta \phi = -\arctan(2\pi f_c R_{\text{ESR}} C)$ Loop Trip Limit: $45^\circ$
03 / Thermal Balance Active Actuation

Electro-Thermal Lumped Energy Conservation

CryoESR converts the parasitic ESR rise into an internal heating actuator. By perturbing the DC-DC switching frequency $f_{sw}$ or inducing controlled AC inter-phase circulating ripple current $I_{\text{ripple}}$, internal heat is generated directly inside the frozen electrolyte core without external heater mass:

$$C_{th} \frac{d T_{core}}{dt} = I_{\text{RMS, rip}}^2 \cdot R_{\text{ESR}}(T_{core}) - G_{th}(T_{core} - T_{sink}) - \varepsilon \sigma_{SB} A_{s} (T_{core}^4 - T_{rad}^4)$$

Where $C_{th}$ is thermal capacitance of the capacitor pack ($J/\text{K}$), $G_{th}$ is thermal conductance to the cryogenic cold-plate, and $T_{sink}$ is the Lunar PSR environment ($-180^\circ\text{C}$ to $-230^\circ\text{C}$). The nonlinear positive feedback term $I_{\text{rip}}^2 R(T)$ ensures maximum heating efficiency exactly when $R$ is high.

Thermal Time Constant: $\tau_{th} = C_{th} / G_{th} \approx 42.4\text{ s}$ Zero Mass Penalty
04 / Observer Synthesis Kalman Inversion

Dual Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) State Estimator

Direct capacitor core thermocouple probes are flight failure points. CryoESR implements an online dual Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) that measures the high-frequency switching voltage ripple $\Delta V_{out}(t)$ and inductor current ripple $\Delta I_L(t)$ from standard telemetry to extract real-time $R_{\text{ESR}}$ and reconstruct core $T_{core}$:

$$\hat{R}_{\text{ESR}}[k] = \frac{\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} \Delta v_{out}[k-n] \cdot \Delta i_C[k-n]}{\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} (\Delta i_C[k-n])^2}, \quad \hat{T}_{core} = T_0 + \frac{B}{k_B \ln\left( \frac{\hat{R}_{\text{ESR}} - R_m}{\alpha_0} \right)}$$

The observer runs deterministically in flight microcontrollers at a $2.5\text{ kHz}$ update rate. If $\frac{d\hat{R}_{\text{ESR}}}{dt} > 0$ while estimated core margin $\hat{T}_{core} - T_g < 25\text{ K}$, the adaptive ripple injection loop is automatically engaged.

Estimation Accuracy: $\pm 1.2\text{ m}\Omega$ / $\pm 1.8^\circ\text{C}$ No Physical Thermocouple
